KN5 GARAGE

A garage for game mods and 3D models that runs entirely on your PC: Assetto Corsa cars, BeamNG.drive vehicles, characters, figures and props in 12 formats. Edit, pose, paint, print, convert, and export to Blender, game engines, 3D viewers and 3D printers.

What it does

Your whole collection

Previews, authors, specs, power curves, skins, CSP features, favourites and your own tags.

Export anywhere

glTF (.glb), OBJ, FBX (through Blender) and STL, with presets for renders, game engines and 3D printing.

3D before you export

Paint colours, steering, lights, day and night studios, animations, measuring, pictures and videos.

Edit cars

Power, torque, weight, gearing and turbo, and the specs in the game menus. Save as a new car, or change the original with a backup you can undo.

Your own livery

Paint in Photoshop, Illustrator, GIMP or any image program; the 3D car updates every time you save, then installs as a new skin.

Parts editor

Hide, remove, move, rotate, scale and recolour parts, or swap in parts from other cars and 3D files.

12 3D formats

.kn5, .glb, .gltf, .vrm, .fbx, .dae, .obj, .stl, .ply, .3ds, .pmx and .bvh, read without other programs, rigs and animations included.

Beyond one game

BeamNG.drive vehicles (folders and zip mods) and loose 3D files sit in the library next to Assetto Corsa cars.

Characters

Show the skeleton, pose any bone, play animations: Mixamo FBX, MMD, VRM, BVH motion capture.

3D printing

Real size in mm, repair, wall thickness for thin surfaces, cut into pieces, STL or 3MF.

Textures

DDS, TGA, PSD and more to PNG, JPEG, TGA, DDS or WebP; resize; flip normal maps.

Import

Install mods and skin packs from .zip, .7z and .rar, open .glb / .gltf / .obj / .fbx files, and make a car from a 3D model (beta).

Blender ready

One click opens an export in a studio scene with sorted parts, lights and a camera.

Tidy up

Finds duplicates and identical copies, shows disk space, and lists missing files.

13 languages

English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Polski, Português, Türkçe, Русский, العربية, 日本語, 한국어, 中文.

Private and safe

Runs on your PC only and loads nothing from the internet. Exports never change your cars; edits ask first and keep a backup.

Download and run

Windows, no install

  1. Download KN5Garage-<version>-windows.zip from the project's Releases page (each release lists a SHA-256 checksum).
  2. Unzip it anywhere and double-click KN5Garage.exe. The app opens in your browser.
  3. Keep the small console window open while you use the app; close it to quit. Settings and exports are kept next to the .exe.

From source (Windows, macOS, Linux)

  1. Install Python 3.9 or newer from python.org (on Windows tick "Add Python to PATH").
  2. Double-click KN5 Garage.bat (Windows), or run python -m pip install -r requirements.txt then python app.py.

Using the app

Library

  • Folders: add as many as you like, or paste a path and press Enter: single cars, your content\cars folder, mod folders anywhere. Rescans are fast: unchanged cars come from a cache.
  • Cards or Table, sorted by any column; the stats bar filters with one click; press / to search (your tags included).
  • Favourites and tags: click the ★, and add your own tags in a car's details.
  • Duplicates: Identical finds copies of the same model under any name; Disk space shows what removing them would free.
  • Skin gallery: every skin of every car, searchable by name, driver, team or number.
  • Details: specs, power/torque chart, skins, files, issues, Custom Shaders Patch features, real numbers from an unpacked data folder, Content Manager variants.
  • Compare 2 to 6 cars, and save the library as a CSV spreadsheet.

Export presets

PresetGives you
RenderFull-quality .glb for Blender: clearcoat paint, metallic/roughness from txMaps, glass, chrome and carbon materials, part pivots, animations, and the driver rigged when its bones are in the model.
GameThe car's lower-detail LOD model with 1K textures.
PrintOne STL mesh in millimetres, Z up, for slicers.
OBJ.obj + .mtl + a textures folder, for older 3D tools.
FBXMade by Blender in the background (textures embedded), for Unity and Unreal.

Options: paint colour (tint or solid, with the paint parts chosen automatically or by clicking the car), headlights on, texture size, JPEG textures, a Blender setup script, and saved presets. Each export gets a report, and History keeps past exports with an Again button.

Import

Install downloaded mods and skin packs (.zip; .7z and .rar with 7-Zip): choose what to install and whether to skip, install next to, or replace what is already there. Programs inside archives are never unpacked. Open .glb, .gltf, .obj, .fbx and .kn5 files to view or convert, or make a new car from a 3D model on a donor car's physics, sound and wheels (beta).

Formats and other games

The scan finds Assetto Corsa cars, BeamNG.drive vehicles (folders and zip mods, shown in their default configuration with their skins) and every 3D file the app reads. SketchUp .skb / .skp files aren't public formats: export .dae from SketchUp. Forza Horizon and CarX Drift Racing keep their files encrypted and make liveries in their own editors, so they can't be opened.

3D printing and textures

3D print… makes STL or 3MF at a real height, repaired, with walls for thin surfaces and cut into pieces. Import > Convert textures turns DDS, TGA, PSD and more into PNG, JPEG, TGA, DDS, WebP or BMP.

                           [--paint #c8102e] [--paint-mode tint|solid] [--texture-size 1024]

Privacy and security

  • The app only listens on 127.0.0.1: other computers can't reach it.
  • Websites can't control it: a random per-start token, and cross-site requests, DNS rebinding and framing are blocked.
  • The page loads nothing from the internet: no web fonts, analytics or update checks. Nothing is logged.
  • Paths on screen start with ~ instead of your Windows user name.
  • Exports only read car folders; edits write only when you save, after asking, with a backup.
  • Mod files, archives and 3D files are treated as untrusted: unsafe paths, links, zip bombs and programs inside archives are refused.

Details: SECURITY.md. Some authors protect their cars; KN5 Garage exports only their shape and never tries to break the protection. Please respect mod authors.

Building and contributing

  • run_tests.bat runs the unit, security, web and end-to-end tests.
  • build_exe.bat builds dist\KN5Garage.exe; pushing a tag like v1.3.0 builds, tests and publishes it on GitHub.
  • tools/make_demo_garage.py builds the demo cars; tools/screenshots.py remakes these pictures.
